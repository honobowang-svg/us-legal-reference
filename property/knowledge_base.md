## C. Eviction overview

Judicial process required in all major states for residential eviction (unlawful detainer / forcible entry and detainer). **Illegal lockout** can create landlord liability. TX justice courts hear FED actions (Gov’t Code § 27.031). CA unlawful detainer has specialized statutes and self-help pages on courts.ca.gov.
