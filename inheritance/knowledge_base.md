## A. Will execution formalities (general US pattern)

Most states require a written will signed by the testator and attested by **two witnesses** (exact rules vary; holographic wills allowed in some states including CA/TX with conditions). **Always open the state’s estates/probate code** before stating formalities as verified.

## B. Intestacy (high-level — verify shares)

Intestate succession depends on surviving spouse, descendants, parents, etc. Community-property states (CA/TX) treat **community vs separate** property differently on death.

**Do not invent fractional shares.** Walk the user to the official intestacy statute for their state; summarize only sections actually opened and quoted.

---

## C. Probate vs non-probate transfers

Non-probate: joint tenancy with right of survivorship, beneficiary designations (POD/TOD, life insurance, retirement), revocable trusts. Probate administers probate estate assets.

Small-estate affidavits exist in many states with dollar caps — **[TO VERIFY]** each state’s current cap before quoting.

## D. Creditor claims in probate

States set claim-filing windows after notice/publication. Missing the window can bar claims — cite the state probate code section after verification.
