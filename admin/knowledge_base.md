## A. Immigration & Nationality Act (INA)

Codified primarily in **8 U.S.C.** Implementing regulations in **8 C.F.R.** Always prefer uscis.gov policy manuals and current form instructions over secondary blogs.

---

## B. Common status families (labels only)

- Nonimmigrant visas (B, F, H-1B, L, O, etc.) — categories defined in INA / DOS  
- Immigrant / permanent residence pathways (family, employment, diversity, refugee/asylee adjustment)  
- Naturalization (INA § 316 et seq. concepts)  
- Removal / deportation defense is specialized — **direct to counsel**

**Do not** invent eligibility scores or processing times; cite USCIS processing time tools when discussing wait estimates as non-binding.

---

## C. Administrative Procedure Act (APA) sketch

Agency rulemaking and judicial review concepts (5 U.S.C.) — high-level only; FOIA separate (5 U.S.C. § 552).

---

## D. State administrative law

Licensing boards, unemployment insurance appeals, driver’s license hearings — **state-specific**. After identifying state, point to that state’s APA / agency site.
