# Employment & Wage · Deadlines

---

## 1. FLSA private actions

| Item | Period | Authority |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Ordinary FLSA wage/OT claim | **2 years** from accrual | 29 U.S.C. § 255(a) |
| Willful FLSA violation | **3 years** | 29 U.S.C. § 255(a) |

---

## 2. EEOC charge (discrimination)

| Item | Typical period | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Charge with EEOC | **180 days** or **300 days** if state/local FEP agency | Confirm on eeoc.gov for the user’s state **[re-verify]** |

## 3. State wage claims (major states) — verify locally

| State | Practical note | Verify |
|-------|----------------|--------|
| CA | DLSE / Labor Commissioner claims and civil actions have specific limitations; waiting-time penalty rules interact with timing | Lab. Code; DIR |
| TX | Many wage claims pursued under FLSA or TX Payday Law — check TWC deadlines | TWC |
| NY | NYLL claims; notice of claim issues if public employer | NY Labor Law / DOL |
| FL | State and federal paths; check DBPR/related and FLSA | flsenate.gov |
| IL | IDOL wage claims + FLSA | ILCS / IDOL |

## 4. User-facing script

> “For federal unpaid minimum wage or overtime, the FLSA limitations period is generally two years, or three if the violation was willful (29 U.S.C. § 255). State claims may differ. Consider contacting the DOL Wage and Hour Division and a licensed employment lawyer in your state promptly.”
