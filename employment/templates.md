# Employment & Wage · Templates (reference only)

> Not court-approved forms. Have a licensed attorney review.

---

## Template 1 — Written wage demand

```text
[Date]

Via [email / certified mail]
[Employer legal name]
[Address]

Re: Wage demand — [Employee name], [dates of employment]

I was employed by you as [job title] from [start] to [end/present] in [city, state].

I believe I am owed:
1. Unpaid wages: $[ ] for [period]
2. Unpaid overtime: $[ ] for [period]
3. Other: [ ]

Please pay the total of $[ ] within [10/14] days and confirm in writing.

This letter is not a waiver of any rights under the FLSA or state law.

Sincerely,
[Name]
[Phone] [Email] [Address]
```

---

## Template 2 — FLSA / state agency complaint intake checklist

```text
- Employer legal name / dba / address / phone
- Work location(s) and remote status
- Dates employed; job duties (for exemption analysis)
- Pay rate(s), pay stubs, time records, schedules
- Hours by workweek; lunch/break policy
- Names of supervisors / payroll contacts
- Prior oral/written complaints and responses
```
