## B. UCC Article 2 (state enactments)

UCC is state law as enacted. Official text for reference: state commercial codes (e.g., Cal. Com. Code; Tex. Bus. & Com. Code).  

Key ideas: merchant firm offers; implied warranty of merchantability (UCC § 2-314); fitness for particular purpose (§ 2-315); statute of limitations for sale-of-goods breach often **4 years** (UCC § 2-725) as enacted — **[TO VERIFY]** in the user’s state commercial code.
