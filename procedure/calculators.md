# Civil Procedure & Small Claims · Calculators

## 1. Is small claims available? (decision tree)

1. Identify **state** and **county/city**.  
2. Identify **claimant type** (natural person vs entity).  
3. Identify **amount demanded** (principal; ask whether interest/fees count toward the cap in that forum).  
4. Compare to verified cap:  
   - CA natural person: ≤ **$12,500** (CCP § 116.221)  
   - TX justice court AIC: ≤ **$20,000** excl. interest (Gov’t Code § 27.031)  
   - FL small claims rule amount: commonly ≤ **$8,000** (confirm Rule 7.010) while county court may hear larger sums up to § 34.01  
   - NY: depends on court ($3k / $5k / $10k)  
   - IL: **[TO VERIFY]**  
5. If over cap → regular civil / county / district / superior court.

## 2. Example

Claim $9,000 contract debt, individual plaintiff, Los Angeles County → within CA natural-person small claims ($12,500).  
Same claim by a corporation → check § 116.220 entity limit (often $6,250) — may need limited civil instead.
