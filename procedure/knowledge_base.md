## A. California small claims — natural person limit

### Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 116.221 【Verified · LegInfo 2026-11-02】

**Source:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=116.221  

> In addition to the jurisdiction conferred by Section 116.220, the small claims court has jurisdiction in an action brought by a **natural person**, if the amount of the demand does not exceed **twelve thousand five hundred dollars ($12,500)**, except as otherwise prohibited by subdivision (c) of Section 116.220 or subdivision (a) of Section 116.231.

**(Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 861, Sec. 6. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2024.)**

### Entity / other limits

CA Courts Self-Help: generally sue for up to **$12,500** in small claims (or up to **$6,250** if you’re a business) — https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/ (small claims basics). Confirm § 116.220 text on LegInfo for entity/guarantor nuances. **[§ 116.220 full quote TO VERIFY on LegInfo]**

## B. Texas justice court jurisdiction amount

### Tex. Gov’t Code § 27.031(a)(1) 【Verified · statutes.capitol.texas.gov 2026-11-02】

**Source:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=GV&Value=27.031  

Justice court has original jurisdiction of civil matters in which exclusive jurisdiction is not in the district or county court and in which the amount in controversy is **not more than $20,000, exclusive of interest**; also forcible entry and detainer; certain personal-property lien matters within jurisdiction.

§ 27.031(b) lists exclusions (e.g., divorce; slander/defamation; trial of title to land; etc.).

Small-claims **procedure** in justice court is governed by Texas Supreme Court rules under § 27.060.

## C. Florida county court vs small claims

### Fla. Stat. § 34.01(1)(c) 【Verified · flsenate.gov 2026 Statutes】

**Source:** https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/34.01  

County courts have original jurisdiction of actions at law (except exclusive circuit matters) in which the matter in controversy does not exceed, exclusive of interest, costs, and attorney fees:

1. If filed on or before Dec. 31, 2019: $15,000  
2. If filed on or after Jan. 1, 2020: $30,000  
3. If filed on or after Jan. 1, 2023: **$50,000**  

(CPI adjustment mechanism begins July 1, 2030 per statute text.)

**Small claims:** Florida Small Claims Rules (e.g., Rule 7.010(b)) set a separate **small-claims** monetary ceiling commonly **$8,000** (excluding costs, interest, attorneys’ fees). Confirm the Florida Bar / Florida Courts official PDF current through the user’s filing date. **[Rule 7.010(b) verbatim TO VERIFY on flcourts / Florida Bar PDF]**

## D. New York small claims (court-dependent)

**NY Courts self-help (nycourts.gov/courthelp/smallclaims/):**

- **New York City:** sue for up to **$10,000**  
- **Nassau & Suffolk:** up to **$5,000**  
- **City Courts (outside NYC):** up to **$5,000**  
- **Town and Village Courts:** up to **$3,000**
