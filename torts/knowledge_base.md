## A. California — CCP § 335.1 【Verified · LegInfo】

**Source:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CCP&sectionNum=335.1  

> Within **two years**: An action for assault, battery, or injury to, or for the death of, an individual caused by the wrongful act or neglect of another.

**(Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 448, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)**

## B. Texas — Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code § 16.003 【Verified · capitol.texas.gov】

**Source:** https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/GetStatute.aspx?Code=CP&Value=16.003  

(a) … a person must bring suit for … **personal injury**, forcible entry and detainer, and forcible detainer **not later than two years** after the day the cause of action accrues.  
(b) … injury resulting in death: **two years** after accrual; accrues on death of the injured person.

## F. Negligence elements (common-law teaching frame)

1. Duty  
2. Breach  
3. Causation (cause in fact + proximate)  
4. Damages  

Comparative / contributory fault rules are **state-specific** (pure comparative vs modified). CA is pure comparative negligence by case law (Li v. Yellow Cab) — discuss at high level; **do not invent percentages of fault**.

---

## G. Damages categories (civil reference)

Economic (medical, wage loss), non-economic (pain/suffering — subject to state caps in some jurisdictions, e.g., certain med-mal), punitive (heightened standard; state limits). **Never predict a jury number.**
