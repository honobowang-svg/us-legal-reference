## A. Entity choice (high-level teaching)

| Form | Liability sketch | Formation |
|------|------------------|-----------|
| Sole prop | Owner personally liable | Often DBA filing |
| General partnership | Partners personally liable | Agreement + state rules |
| LLC | Limited liability if formalities observed | Articles/certificate with state SOS |
| Corporation | Limited liability; corporate formalities | Articles of incorporation |

Tax classification (disregarded, partnership, S/C corp) is IRS/tax — point to IRS.gov; not deep tax advice.

## B. Federal IP baselines

### Copyright — 17 U.S.C.

Copyright arises on fixation of original works; registration with U.S. Copyright Office enables certain remedies. Term often life of author + 70 years for individual works — confirm current Title 17 before quoting edge cases. **[Key term provisions TO VERIFY if quoting]**

### Trademark — Lanham Act (15 U.S.C.)

Federal registration via USPTO; common-law rights may exist from use. Search TESS before filing advice.

### Patent — 35 U.S.C.

Utility patents: novelty, nonobviousness, utility; USPTO examination. **Do not** draft claims as “filed patent.”

### Trade secrets — Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C. § 1836 et seq.) + state UTSA enactments
