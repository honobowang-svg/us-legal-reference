## D. Custody / parenting time

Best-interest-of-the-child standards are statutory in each state. Federal **UCCJEA** addresses jurisdiction over custody. Cite state code after user names the state.

## E. Domestic violence — civil protective orders

Each major state has a civil restraining / protective order process (e.g., CA Domestic Violence Prevention Act). Encourage safety planning and local court self-help / hotlines. **Do not** provide illegal “self-help” or revenge tactics. Criminal conduct → may also route tip to `criminal/` rights (report to police; counsel).
